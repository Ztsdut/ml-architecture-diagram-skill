# Roadmap

Potential future directions:

- richer editable field, mesh, PDE, molecule, microscopy, remote-sensing, and geoscience primitives;
- optional `torch.fx` runtime corroboration and tensor-shape propagation;
- JAX/Flax and ONNX adapters;
- visual regression snapshots at manuscript size;
- journal presets for common publication formats;
- optional image-model art-direction proposals followed by editable-vector reconstruction;
- interactive Architecture IR and illustration-plan editing;
- architecture-diff visualization;
- caption generation from verified IR;
- additional grammars for state-space models, hypergraphs, and spatiotemporal GNNs.
